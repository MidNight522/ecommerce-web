The useful stuff

A few burning questions.

Straightforward answers about the catalogue, your account and the checkout.

What does the Scoville rating mean?

Scoville Heat Units (SHU) describe a pepper’s heat. Product cards show the rating when it is available, and the catalogue has heat-level filters to help you compare. Explore the heat guide.

Do I need an account to place an order?

Yes. Sign in or create an account to use the shopping cart and checkout. Your orders are linked to your account so you can find them again later.

Is online payment available?

Not yet. Checkout saves your selected payment method and creates an unpaid order. It does not collect card details or charge you. This is an educational store.

Where can I find my order?

Open your account and select Order History. Successfully placed orders are saved on the server and remain available after you refresh the page.

Open my account →
Why did checkout reject my order?

A product may have sold out, become unavailable or changed price since it was added. Check the error message, return to the catalogue and update the items in your basket before trying again.

Will my basket survive a page refresh?

The basket currently lives in this browser session’s running app. Refreshing the page clears it. Orders that have already been successfully placed stay in your order history.

Can I edit my personal information?

Yes. Go to Account Information, update your details and select Save. The button becomes available when you have made a valid change.

Are the product reviews real customer submissions?

The reviews displayed on product pages are demonstration content for the page layout. Public review submissions are not connected.

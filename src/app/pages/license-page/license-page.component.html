Built to learn. Made with care.

A few notes about this project, its content and the tools that bring it to life.

Educational use

The Scoville Inferno is a demonstration ecommerce application. Product copy, sample reviews and store content are used to illustrate the shopping experience. The site is not currently presented as a live retail service.
